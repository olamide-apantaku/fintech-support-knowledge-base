## Common Customer Issues
| Issue | Likely Cause | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Cannot log in | Wrong password or locked account | Reset password or unlock account |
| Transaction failed | Insufficient funds or network error | Verify balance and retry |
| Account restricted | KYC not completed or flagged | Guide customer through KYC |
| Wrong transfer | Incorrect account details entered | Escalate to disputes team |
| Card declined | Limit exceeded or card blocked | Check limits or unblock card |
| Delayed payment | Bank processing time | Advise 24-48 hour processing time |

## KYC Troubleshooting
KYC (Know Your Customer) is a mandatory verification 
process required by financial regulations.

**Common KYC Issues:**
- Document not accepted — wrong format or expired
- Selfie not matching ID photo
- Address proof not recognised
- KYC stuck in pending status
- Account limited due to incomplete KYC

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Confirm which document was rejected and why
2. Advise customer on accepted document formats
3. Guide customer to resubmit via the correct channel
4. Escalate to compliance team if stuck in pending
5. Follow up within 24 hours to confirm resolution
6. Document all KYC interactions in CRM

**Accepted KYC Documents:**
- Government issued ID — National ID, Passport, Drivers Licence
- Proof of address — Utility bill, Bank statement (not older than 3 months)
- Selfie with ID — Clear photo, good lighting, no filters

## Failed Transaction Scenarios
**Scenario 1 — Insufficient Funds**
- Confirm account balance with customer
- Advise customer to top up and retry
- Check if any pending transactions are holding funds

**Scenario 2 — Network or System Error**
- Confirm if transaction was debited
- If debited and not received — escalate to technical team
- Provide reference number for tracking
- Follow up within 24 hours

**Scenario 3 — Wrong Account Details**
- Do not attempt to reverse without proper verification
- Escalate immediately to disputes team
- Document full transaction details in CRM
- Advise customer on expected resolution timeline

**Scenario 4 — Transaction Limit Exceeded**
- Confirm current transaction limits with customer
- Advise on how to request limit increase
- Guide customer through verification if required

## Password and Security Issues
**Password Reset Process:**
1. Direct customer to forgot password option
2. Confirm registered email or phone number
3. Guide through OTP verification process
4. Confirm successful password reset
5. Advise customer to use strong password

**Account Locked:**
1. Verify customer identity before unlocking
2. Confirm reason for lockout
3. Unlock account from CRM if authorised
4. Advise customer on security best practices
5. Document interaction in CRM

**Security Best Practices to Share with Customers:**
- Never share OTP or password with anyone
- Use unique passwords for financial accounts
- Enable two factor authentication
- Report suspicious activity immediately

## Verification Process
**Standard Verification Steps:**
1. Confirm full name on account
2. Confirm registered email address
3. Confirm registered phone number
4. Confirm last transaction amount or date
5. For sensitive requests — request ID verification

**When to Apply Enhanced Verification:**
- Password reset requests
- Account number changes
- Large transaction disputes
- Account reactivation requests

## Fraud and Dispute Handling
**Signs of Fraudulent Activity:**
- Unusual transaction patterns
- Multiple failed login attempts
- Customer reports unauthorised transaction
- Account accessed from unusual location

**Fraud Response Process:**
1. Take the customer report seriously
2. Immediately flag the account for review
3. Escalate to fraud and compliance team
4. Advise customer to change password immediately
5. Document all details in CRM
6. Follow up within 24 hours with update
7. Never share investigation details with customer

**Dispute Resolution Steps:**
1. Collect full transaction details from customer
2. Verify transaction in system
3. Escalate to disputes team with full documentation
4. Provide customer with reference number
5. Advise expected resolution timeline — 3 to 5 business days
6. Follow up until resolution is confirmed

## Regulatory Compliance
- Always follow KYC and AML (Anti Money Laundering) guidelines
- Never process requests that bypass verification
- Report suspicious transactions to compliance team
- Maintain customer data confidentiality at all times
- Follow data protection regulations — NDPR for Nigeria
- Never share customer information with unauthorised parties
- Document all compliance related interactions

## Communication Templates

**KYC Pending:**
> "Thank you for your patience. Your KYC verification 
is currently under review. This typically takes 
24 to 48 hours. We will notify you once completed."

**Failed Transaction:**
> "I understand how concerning this is. I have 
escalated your transaction issue to our technical 
team. You will receive an update within 24 hours. 
Your reference number is [REF]."

**Account Restricted:**
> "Your account has been temporarily restricted 
to protect your security. To restore full access 
please complete your KYC verification by [method]."

**Fraud Report Acknowledgement:**
> "Thank you for bringing this to our attention. 
I have flagged your account for immediate review 
by our security team. Please change your password 
now and do not share your OTP with anyone."
